A wrong answer in Glossa

Translate: I am reading a book.

Я чита́ю кни́га.

Not quite

Я чита́ю кни́гу.

кни́га is the direct object of чита́ю, so it takes the accusative case. Feminine nouns ending in -а change the ending to -у.

Why switch

What you get when the goal is the language, not the habit.

  • 1

    Rules you can see

    Cases, aspects, genders and tenses are explained up front, with examples, before you practise them. You don't have to reverse-engineer them from mistakes.

  • 2

    Feedback that teaches

    Every answer is graded with a reason. A translation worded differently from the expected answer can be checked by AI, so a correct sentence is not marked wrong for its wording.

  • 3

    Fourteen kinds of exercise

    Translation, gap fills, word forms, listening, dictation, speaking with pronunciation assessment, reading texts and free writing, not only tapping word tiles.

  • 4

    A real measure of your vocabulary

    Practice words uses spaced repetition and counts the words you know reliably against research-based vocabulary sizes for each CEFR level.

Is it for you?

Glossa is for serious learners who want to understand.

If five playful minutes a day is what you want, Duolingo does that well. If you have reached the point where you keep making the same mistakes and don't know why, Glossa is the next step.

What you already know from Duolingo carries over, and the explanations in the early modules fill in the rules behind it.

  • You want to know why a sentence is right, not only that it is.
  • You find streaks and leagues distracting rather than motivating.
  • You are aiming for B2 or C1: reading articles, working or living in the language.
  • You would rather study for 30 focused minutes than tap through lessons to protect a counter.
