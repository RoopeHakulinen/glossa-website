Choosing

Where Glossa fits, and where it doesn't.

Glossa is for adults who want to understand how a language works and keep going past the intermediate plateau. It asks for focused study rather than a few minutes of tapping.

  • Choose Glossaif you want grammar explained, mistakes explained, and a course that goes all the way to C1.
  • Choose Glossaif streaks, leagues and ads get in the way of learning for you.

Honestly

When another app is the better pick.

  • You want a language Glossa doesn't teachGlossa has five courses, all for English speakers.
  • You learn best by ear, away from a screenPimsleur's audio lessons suit commuting and walking.
  • You want corrections from peopleBusuu's community corrects your writing; Glossa's feedback comes from the course and AI.
  • You want a few playful minutes a dayDuolingo is built for exactly that.
