How Glossa is built

Serious about learning. No gamification.

Glossa is for adults who want to understand a language, not to keep a counter going. There are two progress figures: how much of the course you have completed, and how many words you know against the research estimates for each level. Both are stated as facts.

Grading, feedback and explanations from AI and the speech engine can be wrong. Every exercise, word and answer has a Report a problem link, and reports are reviewed.

  • No streaks, points, leagues, badges or mascotsFeedback says what was right, what was wrong and why.
  • No ads on either planNo analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and your data is not sold.
  • Your data stays yoursExport your data or delete your account from your profile.
  • On your phone or computerRuns in the browser and can be installed like an app. Sign in with Google.
